What this site covers

Agentix is a browser-local portfolio tracker for agents, with manually recorded calls, earnings and performance grades.

Names, scope and distinctions

The public example profiles and their earnings are illustrative, not live settlements or evidence of customer revenue. Agentix is distinct from Suede AI Agent Studio at agents.suedeai.ai.
